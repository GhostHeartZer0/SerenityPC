* 1 pre-made graham cracker crust
* 1 can (8 ounces) sweetened condensed milk
* 1/2 cup sour cream
* 1/4 cup melted butter
* 1/4 cup chopped nuts (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).

3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the condensed milk, sour cream, melted butter, and chopped nuts (if using).
4. Pour the mixture over the graham cracker crust and smooth the top.
5. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the edges are set but the center is slightly jiggly.
6. Let cool completely on a wire rack before serving.
7. Drizzle with chocolate fudge sauce, if desired.

**Tips:**

* For a more authentic New York-style cheesecake, use a food processor to whip the cream cheese until it's light and fluffy.
* To make the crust, you can substitute graham cracker crumbs for crushed Oreos.
* If you don't have a springform pan, you can use a regular 8-inch pan and then carefully lift the cheesecake out of the pan after it has cooled.

**Additional Notes:**

* This recipe makes one cheesecake. If you want to make more, simply double the ingredients.
* Leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

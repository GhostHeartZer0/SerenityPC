In the wild, "true" bananas were large, full of hard, black seeds, and difficult to eat.
*   **The Reason:** The bananas we eat today are the result of selective breeding. Humans have selected for mutations that produce large amounts of pulp and almost no seeds. Therefore, a supermarket banana is a human-engineered biological product rather than a wild organism.

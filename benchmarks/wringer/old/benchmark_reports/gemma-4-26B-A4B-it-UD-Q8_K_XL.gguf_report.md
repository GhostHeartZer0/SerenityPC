In botany, a berry is a fleshy fruit produced from a single ovary of a single flower. To qualify as a berry, a fruit must have:
*   A fleshy middle (mesocarp).
*   A fleshy inner layer (endocarp) that surrounds the seeds.
*   Seeds inside the fruit.

### 3. The Culinary Definition (The "Practical" Answer)
In the kitchen and in everyday life, a banana is a **fruit**. It is a tropical, elongated, edible fruit characterized by its high starch content (which turns to sugar as it ripens), its soft texture, and its distinctive peel.

*   **The Anchors (Preservationists):** Attempt to "re-weave" the strings using massive amounts of baryonic matter. They believe in heavy engineering and physical reinforcement of spacetime.

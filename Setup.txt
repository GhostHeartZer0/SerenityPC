Serenity AI - Easy Setup Guide

This guide will get your AI chatbot up and running with full GPU acceleration on Windows.

Step 1: Install Required Software
You only need two things to get started.

Python 3.11 (64-bit):

Go to: python.org/downloads/windows

Download the "Windows installer (64-bit)".

CRITICAL: When you run the installer, check the box that says "Add python.exe to PATH".

Click "Install Now."

NVIDIA Drivers (Current):

Most users already have these. If you play games, you're likely set.

If not, download the latest drivers for your RTX card at nvidia.com/drivers.

Step 2: Run the Smart Setup
We’ve automated the "bridge" to your GPU.

Open your project folder (where main.py and your new setup.py are).

Double-click setup.py.

The script will automatically:

Detect your NVIDIA GPU version.

Download the pre-compiled "GPU Bridge" (llama-cpp-python) from the web.

Install all other necessary libraries

Once it says "Success! All required libraries are locked and loaded," you are done.

Step 3: Launch Serenity AI
Double-click run.bat in your project folder.

Click Settings to select your model from the list.

Click Begin! to load the model into your GPU.

Troubleshooting for Consumers
"No GPU found" during setup: Ensure your NVIDIA drivers are updated. The script uses nvidia-smi to find your card.

App runs slowly: In the Settings, ensure the GPU Layers slider is turned up (usually to the max or around 33-40 for Gemma models) to ensure the work stays on your RTX card rather than your CPU.
